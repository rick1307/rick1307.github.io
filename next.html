How the game works

Rick lives the Sailing Frog's Leap adventure. LEAP gives you a way to play along with it.

Step 1

Watch

Every episode moves Rick's Sailing Frog's Leap adventure forward.

Step 2

Find the Challenge

Each episode will include LEAP Challenges tied to what Rick is doing, seeing, solving, or experiencing.

Step 3

Engage

Answer, notice, contribute, solve, share, or participate in whatever the challenge asks.

Step 4

Earn

Engagement can earn LEAP, badges, or both — depending on the challenge.

Step 5

Build History

Your rank and badge history grow over time, giving you a reason to come back for the next adventure.

Every episode becomes part of the game.

LEAP Challenges are a core part of the program. Rick may ask a question, hide something in an episode, ask the crew to solve a problem, contribute an idea, share a photo, make a prediction, notice a detail, or take part in something happening around the boat.

The challenge can change with Rick's adventure. The point stays the same: watch, engage, and become part of what is happening.

Engagement is the game

LEAP is not meant to reward passive ownership. It is meant to recognize participation.

LEAP creates progression

The LEAP you earn through engagement moves your Sailing Frog's Leap crew rank. A Sailing Frog's Leap Deckhand can become a Bosun, Quartermaster, First Mate, and beyond by continuing to participate.

The point is not to buy your way through the system. The point is to build a history of engagement with Rick's Sailing Frog's Leap adventure.

Badges create memory

Badges recognize the particular moments you were part of: voyages, places, challenges, milestones, useful contributions, and shared experiences.

Two crew members can hold the same rank and still have completely different stories.

Badges make participation cumulative rather than disposable.

A normal comment, answer, or moment of engagement can disappear as the feed moves on. LEAP gives Rick a way to recognize some of those moments so they can become part of your continuing Sailing Frog's Leap crew history instead.

Some moments only happen once

The adventure itself creates meaning that cannot simply be recreated later.

History creates its own scarcity

A founding-crew moment happens once. A canal transit happens when it happens. An arrival, milestone, episode challenge, or voyage chapter belongs to its place in the story.

A badge can matter because the real event happened when it happened — not because scarcity was manufactured around it.

The next one matters

Once your Sailing Frog's Leap crew history begins to build, the next episode is no longer just another video. It is another chance to engage, add to your story, earn recognition, and be there for what happens next.

The game is participation in Rick's Sailing Frog's Leap adventure.

LEAP is the progression and recognition layer underneath it. Challenges create engagement. LEAP tracks progression. Badges preserve history. The voyage keeps creating new moments for the crew to be part of.
