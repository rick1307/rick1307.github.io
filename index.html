What is LEAP?

LEAP turns participation in Sailing Frog's Leap into a persistent crew identity. Your balance determines your crew rank; special badges recognize what you actually did with, for, or alongside the community.

1

Receive a LEAP

Your first LEAP makes you a Deckhand and officially puts you on the crew.

2

Participate

Useful comments, polls, livestreams, community contributions, and special events can earn recognition.

3

Advance

As your LEAP balance grows, your permanent crew rank rises with it.

Get your first LEAP

LEAP lives on the XRP Ledger. The onboarding process will be kept as simple as possible while the crew pilot is rolled out.

1. Set up an XRPL walletXaman is the recommended wallet for the initial LEAP pilot.
2. Add the LEAP trustlineThis tells your wallet that you want to hold the Sailing Frog's Leap token.
3. Join the crewParticipate in Sailing Frog's Leap and receive your first LEAP.
4. Build your historyEarn more LEAP, advance your rank, and collect recognition along the way.
Pilot note: LEAP is being introduced first as a community membership and recognition system. It is not a promise of profit or future monetary value.
