The LEAP Cycle

Watch. Engage. Earn. Build history. Then keep the cycle going.

We use LEAP — a digital crew token on the XRP Ledger. The idea is straightforward, even if the technology behind it isn’t yet. LEAP lets us turn participation in Sailing Frog’s Leap into a durable digital record: who joined, what they earned, where they’ve been, and what they contributed. As you participate, the LEAP you earn advances your crew rank, while badges preserve the moments and contributions that make your history unique.
