LEAP Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

You do not need to be a crypto expert to join the Sailing Frog's Leap crew. Here are simple answers to the questions new crew members are most likely to have.

Getting started

The basics

Do I have to buy LEAP? No. LEAP is a crew membership and recognition token. When you earn LEAP, Sailing Frog's Leap can send it to you by Check.
Why Xaman? Xaman is a wallet made for the XRP Ledger, where LEAP lives. It is the wallet we use for LEAP.
Why do I need XRP? XRP pays the XRP Ledger's small network fees and supports the wallet's reserve requirement. You only need a small amount, but network requirements can change.
Is XRP the same as LEAP? No. XRP is the native asset of the XRP Ledger. LEAP is the Sailing Frog's Leap crew token that lives on the XRP Ledger.
LEAP Checks

About the Checks

What is a LEAP Check? A LEAP Check is an XRP Ledger tool we use to send you a LEAP award. You decide whether to cash it.
Why use Checks instead of just sending LEAP? Checks let you accept each LEAP award yourself. They also give each award a clear record on the XRP Ledger.
Do I need to set up LEAP before I receive a Check? No separate LEAP setup is required before we send the Check. Xaman guides you through what is needed when you cash it.
Do I have to cash a Check? No. It is your wallet. You choose whether to accept the Check.
Can a Check say why I earned it? Yes. A Check can include a short memo that helps record why you received the LEAP. Those notes can become part of your crew history.
Privacy and control

Your wallet stays yours

We never need your wallet secrets.

Sailing Frog's Leap only needs your public Xaman wallet address.

Never send us your recovery words, private key, family seed, or recovery phrase.

Is my wallet address private? No. Your XRP Ledger address and its public transaction history can be seen on the public ledger.
Can Sailing Frog's Leap move my XRP or LEAP? No. You control your wallet with your private keys. We cannot move assets from your wallet.
Should I use my main crypto wallet for LEAP? We recommend that you don't. A separate Xaman account for LEAP and other public community activity keeps that activity separate from your primary crypto wallet.
About LEAP

One more thing

Is LEAP an investment promise? No. LEAP is being introduced as a crew membership and recognition system. It is not a promise of profit or future monetary value.
