Add a Community

There is one normal path. Wild Ledger will generate the new community's permanent 3-character ID, verify its first Community Operating Wallet, and guide you through the Owner-authorized XRPL transaction that establishes both facts together.

Nothing becomes canonical until XRPL validates the provisioning transaction.
New community provisioning

One guided process from start to finish

You do not build the authority memo by hand. You do not choose a Community ID format. You provide the first Operating Wallet and approve the Owner transaction; Wild Ledger handles the protocol details and checks each lock before moving forward.

Canonical birth event
Community ID + first Operating Wallet
Those two facts become canonical together in one validated Owner-signed authority statement.
What happens next

You move through five locks

Each step opens the next. If a prerequisite is not satisfied, Wild Ledger keeps the following action locked.
1
Wild Ledger confirms Owner authority
The page reads validated XRPL history and determines the current Wild Ledger Owner Wallet. This normally clears automatically.
Wild Ledger does this
2
A new Community ID is generated
Wild Ledger creates an unused 3-character ID and its matching -LEAP namespace. You paste the XRPL address that will become the community's first Operating Wallet and press Verify.
ID generated automaticallyYou provide the wallet
3
Connect the current Owner Wallet
Press Connect Bifrost and approve the WalletConnect pairing. Wild Ledger verifies that the connected XRPL account is the Owner Wallet it just reconstructed from XRPL.
You connect BifrostWild Ledger checks the signer
4
Prepare, review, and sign
Press Prepare Provisioning Transaction. Wild Ledger builds the exact no-op AccountSet and shows it to you. Then press Send to Bifrost for Signature and approve it in Bifrost.
Wild Ledger builds the transactionYou approve the signature
5
Record it on XRPL and confirm it
Press Submit Signed Transaction to XRPL. Wild Ledger waits for validation and then reads XRPL again. Only after that confirmation does the community exist canonically.
You submit when readyWild Ledger validates the result

Bifrost with the current Owner Wallet availableThe provisioning transaction must be signed by the canonical Wild Ledger Owner Wallet. The normal XRP network fee applies; no LEAP is transferred.
