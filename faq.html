LEAP Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? Start here.

You do not need to be a crypto expert to join the Sailing Frog's Leap crew. Here are simple answers to the questions new crew members are most likely to have.

The short version: use Xaman Wallet, keep a small amount of XRP in it, give us your public wallet address, and cash the LEAP Checks we send you.

You earn LEAP.

We send a LEAP Check to your Xaman Wallet.

You cash it in Xaman.

The XRP Ledger keeps the record.

Getting started

Joining the crew

What do I need to join? Start with Xaman Wallet on your mobile device. You also need a small amount of XRP in the wallet. Then send Sailing Frog's Leap your public Xaman wallet address.
Why Xaman? Xaman is a mobile wallet made for the XRP Ledger. It is the wallet we are using for the LEAP pilot.
What makes me a Deckhand? Cash your first official LEAP Check. When the 1 LEAP reaches your wallet, you are a Deckhand.
Do I have to buy LEAP? No. LEAP is being used as a crew membership and recognition system. If you earn LEAP, Sailing Frog's Leap can send it to you by Check.
XRP and network costs

Why do I need XRP?

Do I need XRP to use LEAP? Yes. Keep a small amount of XRP in your Xaman Wallet. XRP is used for the XRP Ledger's very small network fees and for the wallet's reserve requirement.
How much XRP do I need? You do not need much, but the XRP Ledger's requirements can change. Keep a small amount available in Xaman so you can use the network and cash your LEAP Checks.
Is XRP the same as LEAP? No. XRP is the native asset used by the XRP Ledger. LEAP is the Sailing Frog's Leap crew token that lives on the XRP Ledger.
Is the network fee large? XRP Ledger transaction fees are normally very small. The important point is simply that your wallet needs some XRP available to use the network.
LEAP Checks

How the Checks work

What is a LEAP Check? It is an XRP Ledger tool that lets Sailing Frog's Leap offer LEAP to your Xaman Wallet. You decide whether to cash it.
Why use Checks instead of just sending LEAP? A Check lets you choose to accept each LEAP award. It also gives each award a clear record on the XRP Ledger.
Do I need to set up LEAP first? No separate LEAP setup is needed for the Check onboarding path. Open the LEAP Check in Xaman and cash it.
Do I have to cash a Check? No. It is your wallet. You choose whether to accept the Check.
How do I get more LEAP? Take part in the crew. If you earn more LEAP, Sailing Frog's Leap sends another Check to your Xaman Wallet.
Can a Check say why I earned it? Yes. We can use a short memo such as LEAP-JOINED, LEAP-PANAMA-CANAL, or LEAP-PROBLEM-SOLVER. That helps build your crew logbook.
Ranks and badges

What happens after I join?

What is my rank? Your rank is based on how much LEAP you currently hold. Your first LEAP makes you a Deckhand.
Can my rank change? Yes. If your LEAP balance changes, your current rank can change too.
What is a badge? A badge records something that happened: a voyage, a place, a milestone, a useful contribution, or another crew event.
What is the difference between rank and badges? Rank tells where you stand now. Badges tell where you have been and what happened along the way.
Can a Deckhand have a rare badge? Yes. Rank and badges are separate. A new Deckhand could have a special badge that another high-ranking member does not have.
Wallet safety and privacy

Keep control of your wallet

Never share your wallet secrets.

Sailing Frog's Leap only needs your public Xaman wallet address.

Never send us your secret words, private key, family seed, or recovery phrase.

If anyone asks you for those things, do not give them out.

Is my wallet address private? No. Your XRP Ledger address and its public transaction history can be seen on the public ledger.
Can Sailing Frog's Leap move my XRP or LEAP? No. You control the wallet with your private keys.
Should I use my main crypto wallet for LEAP? I recommend that you don't. Use a separate Xaman account for LEAP and other public community activity rather than your primary crypto wallet. That keeps your LEAP membership activity separate from the wallet you use for other crypto activity.
The bigger idea

Why put this on a blockchain?

Why not just keep a list on a website? A website list belongs to the website. The XRP Ledger gives the membership and transaction history a shared public record.
What is the crew logbook? It is the history created by your LEAP Checks, memos, balances, ranks, and badges.
How could AI use this later? Software or AI can read public ledger data. It could help check rank, find badges, show your history, or unlock a Discord role without someone reading every transaction by hand.
Is LEAP an investment promise? No. LEAP is being introduced as a crew membership and recognition system. It is not a promise of profit or future monetary value.
