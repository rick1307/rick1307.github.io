LEAP Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

You do not need to be a crypto expert to join the Sailing Frog's Leap crew. Here are simple answers to the questions new crew members are most likely to have.

Getting started

The basics

Do I have to buy LEAP? No. LEAP is a crew membership and recognition token. When you earn LEAP, Sailing Frog's Leap can send it to you by Check.
Why Xaman? We use Xaman because it supports the XRP Ledger Check system we use to distribute LEAP. Xaman gives you a clear way to receive and cash a LEAP Check and handles the LEAP trust-line setup when needed. Using one wallet also lets us give every new crew member the same instructions.

The XRP You Need

Why do I need XRP? When you use Xaman, you control an account on the XRP Ledger. That account needs a small amount of XRP to operate. XRP provides the reserve required by the ledger and pays the tiny network fees when you sign transactions.
How much XRP do I actually need for LEAP? Not much. For LEAP, a few XRP is plenty. You do not need to put a meaningful amount of money into the wallet.
Are XRP transaction fees expensive? No. At the XRP Ledger's current standard base fee, an ordinary transaction costs about 0.00001 XRP. At that rate, 0.01 XRP could cover roughly 1,000 ordinary transactions. Most of the XRP in the wallet is there to satisfy ledger reserve requirements, not because LEAP transactions are expensive.
Am I being asked to invest in XRP? No. You do not need to speculate on XRP or become a crypto investor to join the crew. For LEAP, XRP is simply the native asset that keeps your XRP Ledger account operating.
Is XRP the same as LEAP? No. XRP is the native asset of the XRP Ledger. LEAP is the Sailing Frog's Leap crew token that lives on the XRP Ledger.
LEAP Checks

About the Checks

What is a LEAP Check? A LEAP Check is a payment feature built into the XRP Ledger. We use it to offer you LEAP without simply pushing tokens into your wallet. You choose whether to cash the Check and accept the LEAP.
Why use Checks instead of just sending LEAP? Checks let you choose whether to accept each LEAP award. They also let Xaman handle the trust-line setup when needed and give each award its own record on the XRP Ledger.
Do I need to set up LEAP before I receive a Check? No separate LEAP setup is required before we send the Check. Xaman guides you through what is needed when you cash it.
Do I have to cash a Check? No. It is your wallet. You choose whether to accept the Check.
Can a LEAP Check record why I earned it? Yes. When we create a LEAP Check, we can use the CheckCreate transaction's standard XRP Ledger Memos field to record why the Check was created — for example, LEAP-PANAMA-CANAL or LEAP-FOUNDING-CREW. If no memo is needed, the Memos field can simply be omitted.
Badges

How badges work

What is a badge? A badge records something you did, experienced, or contributed with Sailing Frog's Leap — a voyage, a place, a milestone, a useful contribution, or another meaningful crew event.
What's the difference between a rank and a badge? Your rank reflects how much LEAP you currently hold. A badge records something you did, experienced, or contributed. Your rank can change; your badge history records your story.
How do I earn a badge? Badges recognize specific things you do, experience, or contribute with Sailing Frog's Leap. Some may mark voyages or places, while others recognize participation, contributions, milestones, or special moments. You do not collect them by buying them; they are awarded when something worth remembering happens.
Does earning a badge always mean more LEAP? Not necessarily. The LEAP amount associated with a badge can be small and symbolic. The important part is the recognition and the history it records.
Who decides when a badge is earned? During the pilot, Sailing Frog's Leap defines the badge rules and awards recognition. If the system grows, some recognitions could later be automated.
Technical details

Under the hood

You do not need to understand any of this to use LEAP. This section is for anyone interested in how LEAP uses the XRP Ledger under the hood.

Memos & Transactions

Where does the memo actually live? The memo belongs to the CheckCreate transaction that creates the Check. It does not belong to an individual LEAP token or to the Check object itself. When the recipient later cashes the Check, that is a separate CheckCash transaction. The original memo remains in the public history of the CheckCreate transaction.
Does the memo travel with one specific LEAP coin? No. LEAP is fungible. The memo belongs to the transaction record, not to one individually serialized LEAP unit.

LEAP Naming Convention

Why does each LEAP memo start with LEAP-? LEAP- acts as a namespace — a consistent label that identifies the memo as part of the LEAP system. For example, LEAP-PANAMA-CANAL can be distinguished from unrelated memos in the same wallet's transaction history.
Does the XRP Ledger understand what LEAP- means? No. The XRP Ledger simply stores the memo data. LEAP- is a data convention we chose. Its meaning comes from the way we consistently use it.
What does that convention let us do later? Software can retrieve transaction history and identify memo data that begins with LEAP-. Those records can then be filtered, indexed, and organized into things such as a crew logbook or badge history. If the records are indexed in a database, they can be queried and grouped much like other structured data. The XRP Ledger stores the record; the indexing and application layer provides the SQL-like data manipulation.

Badges on the Ledger

How is a badge actually recorded on the ledger? When Sailing Frog's Leap awards a badge through a LEAP Check, we record the recognition in the Memos field of the CheckCreate transaction. The memo uses a standardized LEAP name, such as LEAP-PANAMA-CANAL or LEAP-FOUNDING-CREW.
What does the badge memo do? It gives the recognition a consistent, machine-readable name so software can identify and classify that event later from the public ledger history.
Does that badge record remain on the XRP Ledger? Yes. Once the CheckCreate transaction is validated, it remains part of the public XRP Ledger history. Cashing the Check later is a separate CheckCash transaction; it does not remove or replace the original badge record.
How could these records become a crew logbook later? Software can retrieve the public transaction history, identify the standardized LEAP records, and organize them into a human-friendly profile showing badges, voyages, milestones, and links back to the underlying ledger records.
About LEAP

What LEAP Is — and Isn’t

Is LEAP an investment? LEAP is being created as a crew membership and recognition system, not as an investment product. Sailing Frog's Leap does not promise that LEAP will increase in value, become liquid, or develop a market.
Is Sailing Frog's Leap creating a market for LEAP? No. Sailing Frog's Leap is not creating or funding an AMM, liquidity pool, or other trading market for LEAP. Our purpose in issuing LEAP is to build the crew membership and recognition system described on this site.
Could someone else create a market for LEAP someday? Potentially. The XRP Ledger supports decentralized trading and AMMs for compatible issued tokens. Depending on LEAP's ledger settings, third parties who hold LEAP may be able to create offers or provide liquidity without Sailing Frog's Leap creating or operating that market. If that happens, it would be a third-party market — not the purpose of the LEAP membership program.

Wallet Safety

Protecting Your Wallet

What information does Sailing Frog's Leap need from me? Only the public XRP Ledger address for the Xaman account you use for LEAP. We never need your private keys, recovery words, or other wallet secrets.
Is my XRP Ledger address private? No. Your address and its transaction history are public on the XRP Ledger. That is one reason we recommend using a separate Xaman account for LEAP and other public community activity.
Can Sailing Frog's Leap move my XRP or LEAP? No. You control the keys used to authorize transactions from your account. Sailing Frog's Leap cannot move assets from it.
Should I use my main crypto wallet for LEAP? We recommend that you don't. A separate Xaman account keeps your public LEAP activity separate from your primary crypto holdings.

Never share your wallet secrets.

Sailing Frog's Leap will never ask for your recovery words, private key, family seed, recovery phrase, or other secret credentials.
