Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

These answers cover how LEAP works inside this community. For wallets, Xaman, privacy, transfers, Checks, and shared XRP Ledger mechanics, see the Wild Ledger FAQ.

Community

Joining the community

What is LEAP?LEAP is the shared membership and recognition system used by Wild Ledger communities. Inside this community, LEAP is how participation, recognition, and earned progress can become part of your community record.
Who decides how this community uses LEAP?The community operator decides what participation, recognition, badges, challenges, events, and earned progress mean here. Wild Ledger provides the shared structure underneath; the community decides how to use it.
How does rank work?Rank is based on the LEAP officially earned from this community, not simply the LEAP currently sitting in your wallet. Sending LEAP elsewhere does not erase the rank you already earned here. See this community's ranks →
Where are the wallet, Xaman, privacy, transfers, and Check instructions?Those mechanics apply across Wild Ledger communities, so they live in the Wild Ledger FAQ.
Community Lounge

The community Lounge

What is the community Lounge?The community Lounge is a live gathering place. Members can drop in, choose a temporary alias, and talk with whoever is there.
How do I enter?Enter through your Member Record. The Member Record confirms your community membership before opening the community Lounge.
Does the conversation become part of my Member Record?No. Lounge conversation is temporary. If the community separately recognizes something you did with an official LEAP award, that recognition can become part of your community history.
Where can I learn about aliases and privacy?Those protections are part of Wild Ledger and apply across participating communities. See the Wild Ledger FAQ for how Lounge identity and XRP Ledger identity are kept separate.
Member Record

Your community history

What does my Member Record show?Your Member Record separates what you currently hold from what you have officially earned here. It can show your current LEAP balance, earned LEAP, rank, outstanding LEAP Checks, and the community history recorded for you on the XRP Ledger.
What is the difference between Current LEAP and Earned?Current LEAP is the LEAP in your wallet right now. Earned is the cumulative LEAP officially awarded to you by this community and recognized in your validated history. Rank follows Earned, not your current wallet balance.
When does a recognition count as earned?A Check-based recognition becomes part of your earned community history after that Check is successfully cashed. A Check that is still waiting or that was canceled does not count as earned.
What happens to old canceled Checks?They remain part of the public XRP Ledger transaction history, but they are not shown as earned recognitions in your personal Member Record.
Badges

How badges work

What is a badge?A badge is an open-ended recognition. It has no natural expiration date, so the same badge can keep being awarded for as long as the community operator considers it meaningful.
Does rank control which badges I can have?No. Rank follows the LEAP officially earned from this community, while badges record specific recognitions. A member at one rank can have a badge that a higher-ranking member does not.
Can many people earn the same badge?Yes. A badge can recognize one person or many people over time. The important distinction is that the badge itself stays open-ended rather than closing like a challenge or being tied to one fixed event window.
Where can I see this community's badges?See the Badges page →
How is a badge recorded?A community can record a badge as part of an official LEAP award. Once that award is accepted and validated, the badge can become part of that member's earned community record.
Does the badge record stay on the XRP Ledger?Yes. The underlying transaction remains part of the public XRP Ledger history, and the Member Record can interpret that history to show the badges the member has actually earned.
Challenges

How challenges work

What is a challenge?A challenge is a temporary invitation to go do something specific. It opens for participation and eventually closes.
Does a challenge need a fixed end date?No. A challenge is temporary, but its ending can be known in advance or decided later. It might close on a date, after a goal is reached, after a certain number of completions, or when the community operator decides it has run its course.
Can many people complete the same challenge?Yes. A challenge can recognize many members while it remains open. One person's completion does not automatically end it unless that is how the operator designed the challenge.
Where can I see active and completed challenges?See the Challenges page →
Events

How events work

What makes something an event?An event is tied to a particular day or defined window of time. It can happen in person or online, but the occurrence itself has a known beginning and end.
Can many people be recognized for the same event?Yes. Many members can participate in the same event. They are all being recognized for taking part in that same fixed occurrence.
What happens after the event ends?The opportunity to participate is over, but the event remains part of the community's history. Recognized participation can remain part of each member's record.
