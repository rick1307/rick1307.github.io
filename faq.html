Sailing Frog's Leap

Joining the crew

What does LEAP mean inside Sailing Frog's Leap?LEAP is the crew membership and recognition system used by Sailing Frog's Leap. One LEAP is enough to make you an official Sailing Frog's Leap Deckhand, and your LEAP balance advances your crew rank.
How does Sailing Frog's Leap distribute LEAP?When you earn LEAP, Sailing Frog's Leap can offer it to you by LEAP Check. Checks let you choose whether to accept the LEAP and preserve the reason for the recognition in the XRP Ledger record.
How soon will my first LEAP Check appear?After you send me your XRP Ledger address on the Join page, check back on your Member Record. Usually within 24 hours, I will have created your LEAP Check. Any LEAP Checks waiting for you to cash will be listed there.
Where are the wallet, Xaman, privacy, and Check instructions?Those rules apply to every LEAP community, so they now live in the Wild Ledger FAQ.
Crew Record

Your Sailing Frog's Leap history

What does my Member Record show for Sailing Frog's Leap?Your Member Record shows your current LEAP balance and Sailing Frog's Leap rank, any outstanding LEAP Checks waiting for you, and the Sailing Frog's Leap history you have actually earned on the XRP Ledger.
When does an SFL recognition count as earned?A Check-based recognition becomes part of your personal Sailing Frog's Leap history after that Check is successfully cashed. A Check that is still waiting or that was canceled does not count as earned.
What happens to old canceled SFL Checks?They remain part of the public XRP Ledger transaction history, but they are not shown as earned badges or recognitions in your personal Member Record.
Badges

How Sailing Frog's Leap badges work

What is a badge?A badge is a permanent crew recognition for something meaningful: a milestone, a contribution, a special status, a voyage, a place, or another part of your story.
Does rank control which badges I can have?No. Rank is based on how much LEAP you currently hold, while badges record your history. A Deckhand can have a special badge that a higher-ranking member does not.
How is a badge recorded?Sailing Frog's Leap can offer a badge through a LEAP Check with a standardized memo naming the recognition. When the recipient cashes that Check, the badge becomes part of that member's earned record.
Does a badge have to add more LEAP?No. The LEAP amount can be small and symbolic. The important part of the badge can be the recognition and the ledger record that documents it.
What does the badge memo do?The memo gives the recognition a consistent name inside the Sailing Frog's Leap namespace, such as SFL-LEAP/BADGE=NEW-MEMBER or SFL-LEAP/BADGE=LEAP-OG, so the badge can be identified later from the ledger history.
Does the badge record stay on the XRP Ledger?Yes. The underlying transactions remain part of the public XRP Ledger history. The Member Record interprets that history and shows the badges the member has actually earned.
How are badges shown in my Member Record?Your Member Record lists each earned badge once. Badge dates are not the point of the personal record; the important thing is that the recognition is part of your crew history.
Who decides when a badge is earned?During the pilot, Sailing Frog's Leap defines the badge rules and awards recognition. If the system grows, some recognitions could later be automated.
SFL Protocol

Sailing Frog's Leap naming convention

The XRP Ledger mechanics are explained in the Wild Ledger FAQ. These are the conventions Sailing Frog's Leap uses for its own community records.

What is the SFL memo format?Sailing Frog's Leap uses the namespace SFL-LEAP followed by a category and value: SFL-LEAP/<CATEGORY>=<VALUE>.
What categories does Sailing Frog's Leap use?The current categories are BADGE, EVENT, and CHALLENGE. Examples include SFL-LEAP/BADGE=LEAP-OG, SFL-LEAP/EVENT=LA-CRUZ-BEACH-BONFIRE, and SFL-LEAP/CHALLENGE=LEAP-001.
Does the XRP Ledger understand what SFL-LEAP means?No. The XRP Ledger stores the memo data. SFL-LEAP is the convention Sailing Frog's Leap uses so its software can recognize and organize SFL records consistently.
Why does Sailing Frog's Leap use a namespace?It lets LEAP software separate Sailing Frog's Leap records from unrelated wallet activity and organize them into badges, events, challenges, the Member Record, and administrative history.
