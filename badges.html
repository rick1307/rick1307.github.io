LEAP Badges & Crew Logbook

Badges

LEAP badges recognize the moments, contributions, voyages, places, milestones, and shared experiences that make one crew member's history different from another's.

Three ideas define the badge system

Badges work best when they are meaningful, scarce enough to matter, and easy to understand.

Idea one

Badges recognize events

A badge should mean something actually happened: a passage, a contribution, a milestone, an event, a useful act, or a shared moment.

Idea two

Badges preserve history

Unlike rank, a badge is not meant to change just because the member's current LEAP balance changes.

Idea three

Badges create identity

Two Quartermasters may hold the same amount of LEAP but have completely different badge histories—and therefore completely different stories.

Types of badges

The system can grow over time, but these categories give LEAP a strong starting structure.

⚓

Voyage & Passage Badges

“I was part of this chapter.”

What they recognize

Important passages, cruising regions, canal transits, crossings, or voyage milestones connected to the channel.

  • Panama Canal
  • Mexican Riviera
  • San Francisco Departure
  • Caribbean Passage
📍

Port & Place Badges

“This place is in my logbook.”

What they recognize

Specific ports, anchorages, marinas, regions, or memorable locations in the Sailing Frog's Leap story.

  • Shelter Bay
  • Golfito
  • Bahía del Sol
  • La Cruz
🧭

Participation Badges

“I showed up and took part.”

What they recognize

Community participation that matters: livestreams, polls, special events, recurring involvement, or helping shape a channel decision.

  • Founding Crew
  • Live Watch Crew
  • First 100 Members
  • Launch Day Crew
🛠️

Contribution Badges

“I helped make something better.”

What they recognize

Useful contributions to the channel or community: information, problem-solving, technical help, ideas, research, or helping another crew member.

  • Problem Solver
  • Chart Table Contributor
  • Crew Helper
  • Useful Intel
🏁

Milestone Badges

“I was here when it happened.”

What they recognize

Moments in the history of Sailing Frog's Leap itself.

  • LEAP Genesis
  • First Discord Crew
  • Channel Milestone
  • First LEAP Pilot
🐸

Special Recognition Badges

“This one has a story behind it.”

What they recognize

Rare or one-off recognitions that do not need their own permanent category.

  • Captain's Recognition
  • Above & Beyond
  • OG LEAP
  • Special Expedition Crew
