LEAP

The official crew credential of Sailing Frog's Leap.

A YouTube subscriber can watch from the sidelines. A crew member has a history.

LEAP is a simple way to recognize the people who comment, contribute, help, participate, show up, and become part of the story of Sailing Frog's Leap.

The blockchain is just the logbook. The point is the crew.

How it works

LEAP is designed to be easy to understand even if you have never owned a crypto token before.

Step one

Get your first LEAP

One LEAP is enough to make you an official Deckhand.

Step two

Take part

Useful comments, livestream participation, ideas, questions, and community contributions can earn recognition.

Step three

Build a history

More LEAP advances your rank. Special badges remember what you actually did along the way.

Your place on the crew

Rank is quantity. Badges are history.
