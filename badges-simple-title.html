LEAP Badges & Crew Logbook

Your badges are the story.

LEAP badges recognize the moments, contributions, voyages, places, milestones, and shared experiences that make one crew member's history different from another's.

A badge is not another rank. It is a permanent piece of the member's story.

Rank answers a simple question: Where does this crew member stand right now?

Badges answer a different question: What happened along the way?

A rank can rise or fall with current LEAP holdings. A badge is meant to preserve history.

Three ideas define the badge system

Badges work best when they are meaningful, scarce enough to matter, and easy to understand.

Idea one

Badges recognize events

A badge should mean something actually happened: a passage, a contribution, a milestone, an event, a useful act, or a shared moment.

Idea two

Badges preserve history

Unlike rank, a badge is not meant to change just because the member's current LEAP balance changes.

Idea three

Badges create identity

Two Quartermasters may hold the same amount of LEAP but have completely different badge histories—and therefore completely different stories.

How a badge becomes part of the blockchain logbook

The mechanic can be remarkably simple.

1

Something happens

A member earns recognition for a passage, contribution, milestone, place, or event.

2

LEAP records it

A small symbolic LEAP transfer is sent to the member's wallet.

3

The memo names it

For example: LEAP-PANAMA-CANAL or LEAP-FOUNDING-CREW.

4

The history stays

The transaction remains in the public XRPL history as a permanent record of that recognition.

Rank and badges answer different questions

That separation is what makes the system interesting.

RankWhat is this member's current status?
BadgeWhat happened in this member's history?
Rank can change?Yes. It follows current LEAP holdings.
Badge can change?The intention is that historical recognition remains part of the logbook.
Rank is shared?Many members may have the same rank.
Badge history is shared?Potentially not. The combination of badges can make each member's crew history unique.

Common questions

The pilot can evolve, but these are the principles behind the system.

Does a badge have to add more LEAP? No. The amount can be tiny and symbolic. The important part can be the permanent transaction record and standardized memo.
Does the memo travel with one specific LEAP coin? No. LEAP is fungible. The memo belongs to the transaction, not to one individually serialized LEAP unit.
Can a Deckhand have a rare badge? Absolutely. Rank and badge history are deliberately separate.
Can badges unlock things too? They could. A future Discord or website could recognize a particular badge and unlock a channel, event, poll, or special experience.
Who decides when a badge is earned? During the pilot, Sailing Frog's Leap can define the rules manually. If the system grows, some recognitions could later be automated.
Why not make every little action a badge? Because badges become more meaningful when they remain selective enough to tell a real story rather than becoming noise.
