A note for the next builder

Where you fit.

Your dad has spent a lot of time learning the XRP Ledger and building LEAP. He understands the token, the issuer, the distributor, the ranks, and the idea of turning participation into a permanent crew identity.

The part he does not understand nearly as well is the younger-generation community layer: Discord, online culture, how people actually hang out together, what feels natural, and what feels like friction. That is where you could be unusually useful.

This is really two systems.

LEAP becomes much more interesting when you separate the permanent membership state from the place where the community actually lives.

The ledger layer

LEAP / XRPL

This is the permanent record. Who owns LEAP? How many? What rank does that imply? What badges or history has someone earned? The blockchain is the logbook.

The social layer

Discord / community

This is where the membership becomes useful. Conversation, private channels, polls, events, status, jokes, recognition, early access, and the feeling of actually being part of something.

The opportunity is the bridge between them.

Imagine somebody earns 10 LEAP and becomes a Quartermaster. Their wallet already proves that. Discord could simply reflect it.

Connect wallet → verify ownership → read LEAP balance → assign Discord role. The blockchain says who they are. Discord changes what they can do.

What that could look like

Not everything has to be automated on day one.

Viewer earns LEAP They become part of the crew.
→
Wallet proves balance No passwords or private keys.
→
Discord reflects rank Deckhand, Bosun, Quartermaster, etc.

Your role could start very simply.

This does not need to begin as a software project. It could begin as community design.

1

Build the clubhouse

Design the Sailing Frog's Leap Discord: channels, roles, permissions, voice rooms, culture, naming, moderation, and the things that make it feel alive.

2

Be the usability filter

Tell us where the whole system feels old, awkward, confusing, too crypto-heavy, or simply like something people your age will not bother doing.

3

Help automate what works

If people actually care about Discord ranks and access, then we build the wallet connection, automatic role assignment, badges, and member tools.

Do it in phases.

The point is to learn before overbuilding.

Phase 1 — Manual Open the Discord. Create Deckhand, Bosun, Quartermaster, First Mate, and Master of the Leap roles. Verify LEAP balances manually and assign roles by hand.
Phase 2 — Learn Watch what people actually use. Which channels matter? Do people care about rank? Do they want private rooms, polls, live chat, events, or something we have not thought of yet?
Phase 3 — Automate Build a small LEAP bot/app that links a Discord user to an XRPL wallet, checks LEAP balance, and assigns the right role automatically.
Phase 4 — Productize Once it works for Sailing Frog's Leap, turn the system into a repeatable creator package: token + ranks + website + Discord + wallet verification + community tools.
“Dad is building the membership infrastructure. You could help build the place where the membership actually feels real.”

That is the simplest way to think about it.

Why this could become bigger than one channel

If LEAP works, the next creator may not need LEAP at all.

Imagine the same system for other communities.

A woodworking channel might have NAIL. A fishing channel might have HOOK. A guitar channel might have RIFF. The token changes, but the machinery underneath is similar: membership, ranks, badges, Discord roles, access, and community identity.

The valuable thing may eventually be the system, not the individual token.

What we would want from you first

Not code. Not a commitment. Just your eyes.

Question one

Would your generation actually use this?

What would make it cool? What would make it lame? What would make somebody participate instead of ignoring it?

Question two

What should Discord do?

If LEAP proves membership, what experiences should that membership unlock? What would make the Discord worth joining and staying in?
