Starting allocation

1,000 LEAP

Your community begins with 1,000 LEAP ready to recognize participation through badges, events and challenges.

LEAP is meant to move.

Your starting LEAP is a distribution pool for your community. As you put LEAP into the hands of your members, you create room for your community's LEAP supply to grow.

Replenishment

Put LEAP into your community. Get more LEAP to distribute.

After your initial 1,000 LEAP, replenishment is based on circulation. Whenever you want a plus-up, Wild Ledger looks at how much LEAP you have distributed since your last replenishment. Your new allocation is 110% of that amount.

Why it works

The system rewards engagement with more capacity for engagement.

You decide what matters.

Your community decides what deserves recognition. Wild Ledger does not judge your badges, events or challenges.

Circulation creates capacity.

The more LEAP you distribute, the more LEAP becomes available for you to put back into your community.

The ledger keeps the record.

Your management tools make distribution and replenishment easy to see. The XRP Ledger provides the underlying public record.

What happens after you distribute LEAP?

It belongs to the member who received it. Wild Ledger measures distribution out of your community's official distributor. It does not tell your members what they must do with the LEAP they have received.
