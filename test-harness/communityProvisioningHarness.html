About this test

When do I use this harness? Use it when you need to isolate direct community provisioning from the normal request and setup-fee workflow. This harness creates a real canonical community if Step 6 validates.

XRPL write: the final step submits an Owner-signed AccountSet to XRPL. No LEAP moves and no account setting is changed, but the normal XRP transaction fee applies and validated history is permanent.
