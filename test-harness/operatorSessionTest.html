Practice harness only. Steps 1–4 prove wallet control and resolve current Community Operating Wallet authority from validated XRPL history. Step 5 writes the current Wild Ledger browser session keys from that verified result. Step 6 hands that verified browser session to the real community page without putting role or Community ID in the URL. This is the current static-site scaffold, not the future server-side production authorization boundary. It does not publish community settings or submit anything to XRPL.
