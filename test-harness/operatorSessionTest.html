        About this test
        

          
When do I use this harness? Use it when you want to isolate the complete Operator entrance path: wallet connection, wallet proof, current community authority, browser session creation, and the final handoff into the real community page.

          
How to read the status: yellow means not done, the spinning ring means working, a green check means the step completed correctly, and a red X means stop and use the diagnostic report. This harness does not submit an XRPL transaction.
