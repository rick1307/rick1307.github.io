Connect the wallet you intend to operate with

There is no address-entry box. The XRPL wallet you connect here is the wallet Wild Ledger will provision as the community's first Community Operating Wallet.

This choice matters. Connect a wallet you control and expect to keep. Wild Ledger does not hold your private keys and cannot simply replace an established Operating Wallet if you lose access.
