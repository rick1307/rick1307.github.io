What would this tell us?

If holders rise faster than crew, LEAP is moving beyond the organized community. If the two lines stay close, most possession remains inside the crew. Neither is automatically “better”; the relationship is the useful signal.

What is worth remembering?

The design question is not how much data can fit in a breadcrumb. It is which observations are meaningful enough that we will still want to reconstruct them years later.
