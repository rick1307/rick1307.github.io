Transfer community authority to another wallet

Changes an established community’s canonical Operating Wallet. The currently authoritative Operating Wallet signs a new statement naming its replacement.

Use it whenYou intentionally want to move Community Operating Wallet authority from the current wallet to a different XRPL wallet.
Successful finishThe replacement statement validates and a fresh authority read resolves the new wallet as current.
Writes to XRPL and changes authority. Confirm both the community and replacement wallet before signing and submitting.
Open Harness →changeCommunityOperatingAccount.html

Writes to XRPL. This is not a routine debugging action and should not be repeated after canonical Owner authority already exists.
