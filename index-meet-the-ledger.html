Badges tell the story behind the rank.

Rank shows where a member stands today. Badges preserve the moments, places, contributions, passages, milestones, and memories that made their crew history unique. A Deckhand can have a rare badge. A Master of the Leap can still be missing one.

How does the “blockchain logbook” actually work?

A LEAP Check can carry a memo such as LEAP-PANAMA-CANAL. That transaction becomes a permanent, machine-readable event on the XRP Ledger. This is the deeper idea behind LEAP: turning real participation into shared digital state that software—and eventually AI—can read and act upon.

Ready to come aboard?

Joining the LEAP crew does not require you to be a crypto trader or blockchain expert. Start with Xaman Wallet on your mobile device, keep a small amount of XRP available for the network, give us your public Xaman address, and cash the LEAP Check we send you. Your first LEAP makes you a Deckhand.
