LEAP Crew Ranks

Ranks

LEAP ranks are a simple way to recognize how deeply someone has become part of the Sailing Frog's Leap crew. Your current LEAP balance determines your rank. What you actually did along the way is recorded separately through badges and recognition.

The crew ladder

The system deliberately starts simple. Five rank levels create progression without turning LEAP into a complicated game.

1–4 LEAP

Deckhand

“I’m officially part of the crew.”

⚓

What it means

Deckhand is the moment a viewer crosses the line from audience to crew. One LEAP is enough to say: I showed up, I joined, and I'm aboard.

What it may unlock

  • Official recognition as a crew member
  • Entry into crew-only community spaces
  • Eligibility for crew polls, questions, and badges
5–9 LEAP

Bosun

“I’m not just aboard. I keep showing up.”

🪢

What it means

A Bosun is becoming a familiar hand—someone who keeps showing up, participating, helping, and becoming part of the fabric of the crew.

What it may unlock

  • Visible Bosun recognition
  • Expanded access to crew discussions
  • Participation in selected polls and activities
10–24 LEAP

Quartermaster

“I have history here, and my voice matters.”

🧭

What it means

Quartermaster represents an established supporter with a recognizable history in the community. Useful participation matters more than simply being loud or constantly online.

What it may unlock

  • Quartermaster recognition in community spaces
  • Access to member polls and channel questions
  • Priority consideration for selected Q&A and planning discussions
25–50 LEAP

First Mate

“I’m part of the inner crew.”

⛵

What it means

First Mate is an inner-crew rank for members whose long-term participation and repeated contributions have built a meaningful history with the community.

What it may unlock

  • Priority Q&A or question submission
  • Occasional early access to content or announcements
  • Inner-crew discussions, polls, and public recognition
51+ LEAP

Master of the Leap

“I’ve become part of the history of this community.”

🐸

What it means

Master of the Leap is the highest crew rank. At 51+ LEAP, the emphasis shifts away from chasing another title and toward the history the member continues to build.

What it may unlock

  • Highest crew rank and special public recognition
  • Access to selected inner-crew discussions or events
  • Priority participation in experiments and new LEAP features

Three rules keep the rank system honest

Three simple rules keep the ladder clear and useful.

Rule one

Rank follows current holdings

Your current LEAP balance determines your current rank. If the balance changes, the rank can change too.

Rule two

Badges preserve history

Rank can change. Badges preserve the meaningful moments and contributions that happened along the way.

Rule three

Participation beats noise

LEAP should recognize useful involvement—not simply whoever posts most often.
