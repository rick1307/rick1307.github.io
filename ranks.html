Ranks

The rank ladder

Five rank levels create progression without turning LEAP into a complicated game.

Three rules keep the rank system honest

These rules keep rank tied to real participation in the active community.

Rule one

Rank follows earned LEAP

Your rank is based on LEAP officially earned from this community.

Rule two

Earned stays earned

Sending LEAP to another wallet or member can change your current balance, but it does not erase rank you already earned.

Rule three

Each community stands alone

The same wallet can have different ranks in different communities because each community counts only its own official awards.
