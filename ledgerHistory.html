Outstanding official LEAP Checks only. Cashed and canceled Checks disappear automatically. Expired Checks stay here until you cancel them on the XRP Ledger.

This page scans validated XRPL transaction history for the authorized LEAP distributor and extracts recognized SFL-LEAP public memo records. Canceled Checks stay visible in Ledger History, are marked Canceled, and are excluded from the active recognition totals. The Checkbook separately reads the distributor’s current Check ledger objects and matches them to their originating CheckCreate transactions when available.
