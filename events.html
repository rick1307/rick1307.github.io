Events

Events are the shared moments where the LEAP community meets the real Sailing Frog's Leap story. Show up, take part, and the moment can become part of your crew history.

What an event is

An event is a shared moment in the Sailing Frog's Leap story. It has a time, a place or setting, and a clear way to take part.

Idea one

Be there

Events recognize participation in something that actually happened — a meetup, boat day, gathering, project, passage moment, or another announced crew experience.

Idea two

Know the details

Each event should say when and where it happens and what counts as taking part. If an online event counts, the announcement will say so.

Idea three

Remember the moment

When participation is recognized, that Event can become part of a member's LEAP history — separate from a Badge or Challenge.

How events work

The details will vary, but the basic idea stays simple.

1

An event is announced

The time, place, and participation details are posted.

2

You show up

You take part in the event according to the announced details.

3

The moment is recognized

Sailing Frog's Leap records who took part and what event it was.

4

It joins your history

The Event can then appear as part of your LEAP crew record.
