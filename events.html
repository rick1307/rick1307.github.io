Events

Events are shared moments tied to a specific time. Show up, take part, and that moment can become part of your community history.

What an event is

An event is a shared moment tied to a particular day or defined window of time. It may happen in person or online, but it has a clear beginning, a clear end, and a way to take part.

Idea one

Be part of the moment

Events recognize participation in something that happened at a specific time — a meetup, gathering, livestream, project day, or another announced community experience.

Idea two

Know when it happens

Each event should say when it begins, when it ends, where or how it happens, and what counts as taking part.

Idea three

Preserve the moment

Once the event is over, that occurrence is fixed in time. Recognized participation can remain part of a member's LEAP history long after the event itself has ended.

The date is part of what makes it an event.

An event is different from a badge or challenge because the occurrence itself is anchored to a known time. Many people can participate, but they are all participating in that same moment or defined window.

How events work

The details will vary, but the basic idea stays simple.

1

An event is announced

The time, place, and participation details are posted.

2

You show up

You take part in the event according to the announced details.

3

The moment is recognized

The community records who took part and what event it was.

4

It joins your history

The Event can then appear as part of your LEAP member record.

What an event might look like

There are no fixed event types. These are simply examples of moments a community operator might choose to recognize.

For example

Gathering

A meetup, watch party, open house, cruise-in, marina day, or another planned gathering tied to a specific date and time.

For example

Shared online moment

A livestream, premiere, virtual meetup, or another online experience that happens during a defined window.

For example

Project or experience day

A work day, outing, launch, passage day, show, ride, or another real-world experience where participation happens at a known time.
