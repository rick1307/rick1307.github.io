Join the LEAP Crew

Getting aboard is easy.

You do not need to know a lot about crypto. Start with Xaman Wallet on your mobile device. We send a LEAP Check to your Xaman Wallet. You cash the Check in Xaman. Then you are part of the Sailing Frog's Leap crew!

Your first LEAP makes you a Deckhand. Every LEAP you earn after that can come to your Xaman Wallet the same way: by Check.

Think of a LEAP Check like a claim ticket.

Sailing Frog's Leap sends the Check to your Xaman Wallet. You open Xaman and choose to cash it. When you do, the LEAP goes into your Xaman Wallet.

You are in control the whole time.

How to join

There are only a few steps.

1

Start with Xaman Wallet on your mobile device

Xaman Wallet is the mobile app we use for LEAP. It works with the XRP Ledger.

If you already use Xaman, you are ready for the next step.

2

Keep a small amount of XRP in Xaman

XRP is used to pay the XRP Ledger's very small network fees. The XRP Ledger also requires wallets to keep a small amount of XRP in reserve.

You do not need much XRP, but you do need some before you can cash a LEAP Check.

3

Send us your Xaman wallet address

Your public Xaman wallet address tells us where to send your LEAP Check.

It is okay to share your public wallet address. Do not share your secret words or private key.

4

We send a LEAP Check to your Xaman Wallet

Your first Check will be for 1 LEAP.

This is your invite to join the crew of Sailing Frog's Leap.

5

Open the Check in Xaman

Xaman will show the Check. Look at it. Make sure it is for LEAP and that it came from the official LEAP account.

6

Cash the Check in Xaman

Tap the button in Xaman to cash it. Xaman will ask you to approve the action.

When you approve it, the LEAP goes into your Xaman Wallet.

7

Welcome aboard

You now hold 1 LEAP. That makes you a Deckhand.

Your first LEAP is the start of your crew history.

Your first Check is special.

We can add a short note to the Check. That note can say why you got it.

For your first LEAP, the note might say LEAP-JOINED. That gives your crew history a clear starting point.

How you get more LEAP

Every LEAP award can use the same simple process.

You do something worth noting.

Maybe you help the crew. Maybe you join a live event. Maybe you earn a badge. Maybe you take part in something special.

We send another LEAP Check to Xaman.

You open it in Xaman. You cash it in Xaman. Your LEAP balance grows. Your rank may grow too.

Keep your wallet safe.

We only need your public Xaman wallet address.

Never send us your secret words, private key, family seed, or recovery phrase.

If anyone asks for those things, do not give them out.
