Join the Sailing Frog's Leap Crew

Your first LEAP gets you aboard.

LEAP is a crew membership and recognition system built around the Sailing Frog's Leap community. You do not need to be a trader, a crypto expert, or somebody who spends all day thinking about blockchain. You just need to want to be part of the voyage.

Think of the wallet as your crew ID, LEAP as your membership credential, and the blockchain as the logbook that remembers your history.

Most people who watch a YouTube channel remain anonymous viewers. LEAP is an experiment in making participation more permanent.

When you receive your first LEAP, you become a Deckhand. From there, the things you do—the questions you ask, the help you give, the events you participate in, the places and passages you share with the channel—can become part of your crew history.

The goal is not to turn viewers into speculators. The goal is to turn a community into a crew.

You do not need a particular level of sailing or crypto experience to belong here.

How joining works

The technical part sounds more complicated than it actually is.

Set up an XRP Ledger wallet

You need a wallet capable of holding XRP Ledger issued tokens. During the pilot, Xaman is the recommended wallet because it is designed specifically for the XRP Ledger.

Your wallet belongs to you. Sailing Frog's Leap should never need your secret numbers, recovery phrase, or private key.

Add the LEAP trustline

On the XRP Ledger, a trustline simply tells your wallet that it is willing to hold a particular issued asset—in this case, the official LEAP token. Think of it as adding LEAP to the list of things your wallet knows how to receive.

Receive your first LEAP

When your first LEAP arrives, you become a Deckhand. That is the first permanent step in your crew history.

Participate naturally

LEAP is intended to recognize meaningful participation—not reward spam. Good questions, useful comments, livestream participation, helping the community, sharing knowledge, and taking part in special events are the kinds of things that can matter.

Build rank and history

Your current LEAP balance determines your crew rank. Badges and recognition records preserve the things you actually did along the way.

You are not handing your wallet to us.

Your XRP Ledger address is public, much like a public account number used to receive blockchain assets. Your private key or recovery secret is different: it controls the wallet and should remain private.

The LEAP system can read public information such as a wallet's LEAP balance and public transaction history without needing control of the wallet.

What happens after you become a Deckhand?

Receiving the first token is the beginning, not the finish line.

Your rank can grow

Holding more LEAP moves you through Deckhand, Bosun, Quartermaster, First Mate, and eventually Master of the Leap. Rank is meant to show your current place in the crew system.

Your logbook can grow

Badges and memo-based recognition can preserve specific moments: a voyage, a port, a contribution, a launch event, founding membership, or some other piece of shared history.

Common questions before joining

The pilot is meant to be understandable even if this is your first experience with the XRP Ledger.

Do I have to buy LEAP?The membership concept is centered on distribution, participation, and recognition. The exact pilot rules can evolve, but joining the crew is not intended to require somebody to become a token speculator.
Do I need XRP?XRP Ledger accounts use XRP for network requirements and transaction fees. The exact amount required can change over time, so onboarding instructions should point members to current wallet guidance rather than a hard-coded number.
Can people see my wallet?XRP Ledger addresses and their public transaction histories are visible on the public ledger. You should treat the address you use for LEAP as public information.
Can Sailing Frog's Leap take my crypto?Not simply because you join LEAP. Control stays with whoever holds the wallet's private keys. Never give a website, Discord user, or community member your recovery secret or private key.
Can I lose rank?Yes. Rank reflects the LEAP currently held by the membership wallet. Historical badges and recognition are intended to remain part of the logbook.
What if I only want to watch the channel?That's completely fine. LEAP is an optional layer for viewers who want deeper participation and a persistent crew identity.
