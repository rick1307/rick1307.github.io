Join the Sailing Frog's Leap Crew

Join the Crew

Joining is simple. Start with Xaman Wallet on your mobile device. We send a LEAP Check to your Xaman Wallet. You cash it in Xaman. Then you are part of the Sailing Frog's Leap crew!

You do not need to be a crypto expert.

You only need Xaman Wallet, a small amount of XRP for the network, and a public wallet address where we can send your LEAP Checks.

The rest is simple: we send the Check. You choose to cash it.

Step by Step

Everything you need and exactly what happens, in one place.

Phase 1

Get ready

Three quick steps get you ready for your first LEAP Check.

1

Open Xaman Wallet on your mobile device

If you already use Xaman, you are ready. If not, set up Xaman first.

2

Keep a small amount of XRP in Xaman

XRP is used for the XRP Ledger's small network fees. Your wallet also needs some XRP in reserve.

You do not need much. Just make sure there is some XRP in the wallet before you try to cash a LEAP Check.

3

Take your first LEAP

Take a picture of yourself leaping into the air.

Post it on Instagram and tag Sailing Frog's Leap.

Your photo tells us you want to join. When we see your tag, we'll send you a private message and ask for your public Xaman wallet address.

Send your public wallet address to us by DM. You do not need to post it in public.

Nothing secret. We never need your recovery words, private key, family seed, or wallet secret.
You're ready for your LEAP Check
Phase 2

Claim your first LEAP

We send the Check. You verify it, cash it, and come aboard.

4

We send your first LEAP Check

When you send us your public Xaman address by DM, we'll send your first LEAP Check to that address.

Your first Check is for 1 LEAP.

This is your invite to join the crew of Sailing Frog's Leap.

5

Open the Check in Xaman

Verify the Check before accepting it. Make sure it is for LEAP and was sent by the official Sailing Frog's Leap distributor wallet.

Official LEAP Distributor Address rnbmFoUKhnMZ8QCJ6kA5J2uwfP8rm9cUdU

The sending address must match this address exactly. If it does not match, do not cash the Check.

6

Cash the Check in Xaman

Approve the action in Xaman. The LEAP goes into your wallet.

Welcome aboard, Deckhand.

Your first LEAP makes you a Deckhand. After that, each LEAP you earn can come to you the same way: by Check.

You now hold your first LEAP. Your crew story has started.
