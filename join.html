Join the Community

Join

Joining is simple. Start with Xaman Wallet on your mobile device. We send a LEAP Check to your Xaman Wallet. You cash it in Xaman. Then you are part of the community.

Your first LEAP starts your first rank. After that, each LEAP you earn can come to you the same way: by Check.

You do not need to be a crypto expert.

You only need Xaman Wallet, a small amount of XRP for the network, and a public wallet address where we can send your LEAP Checks.

The rest is simple: we send the Check. You choose to cash it.

Cash it

What you need before we send your first Check

Xaman Wallet

Xaman is the mobile wallet we use for LEAP.

A small amount of XRP

XRP pays the XRP Ledger's very small network fees and helps meet the wallet's reserve requirement. You do not need much, but you do need some.

Your public Xaman address

This tells us where to send your LEAP Check.

Nothing secret

We never need your recovery words, private key, or wallet secret.

Step by step

Here is exactly what happens.

1

Open Xaman Wallet on your mobile device

If you already use Xaman, you are ready. If not, set up Xaman first.

2

Keep a small amount of XRP in Xaman

XRP is used for the XRP Ledger's small network fees. Your wallet also needs some XRP in reserve.

You do not need much. Just make sure there is some XRP in the wallet before you try to cash a LEAP Check.

3

Send us your public Xaman wallet address

This is the address where we send your Check.

Your public address is safe to share. Your secret words and private keys are not.

4

We send a LEAP Check to your Xaman Wallet

Your first Check will be for 1 LEAP.

This is your invite to join the community.

5

Open the Check in Xaman

Look at the Check. Make sure it is for LEAP and came from the official LEAP account.

6

Cash the Check in Xaman

Approve the action in Xaman. The LEAP goes into your wallet.
