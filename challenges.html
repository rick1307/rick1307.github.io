Challenges

Challenges turn watching into doing. They give Leapers a clear objective, a reason to participate, and another way to build a history with Sailing Frog's Leap.

What a challenge is

A challenge is a clear invitation to go do something. It has a recognizable objective and, when needed, a time window or simple rules.

Idea one

Do something real

A challenge should ask for action — not just another click. It might involve trying something, making something, helping, exploring, solving, or participating.

Idea two

Know what counts

Every challenge should say what the objective is, who can take part, and what a Leaper needs to do for the challenge to count.

Idea three

Build history

When a completed challenge is recognized, it can become part of that member's LEAP crew history as a Challenge — separate from a Badge or Event.

How challenges work

The exact rules can change from challenge to challenge, but the basic path stays simple.

1

A challenge opens

The objective, any deadline, and what counts are announced.

2

You go do it

The point is participation in the real world or the real community.

3

You show what happened

If the challenge calls for proof or a story, the instructions will say what to share.

4

It joins your history

Once recognized, the Challenge can appear as part of your LEAP record.
