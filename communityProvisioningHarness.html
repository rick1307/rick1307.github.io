Prepare and approve

Build the exact XRPL transaction, review it, and sign

Wild Ledger builds the canonical no-op AccountSet. Bifrost signs the prepared transaction; it does not invent the transaction for you.

Your action: press Prepare Provisioning Transaction, review the preview, then press Send to Bifrost for Signature.Approve the signature in Bifrost only after the Community ID, Operating Wallet, and authority statement shown here are the ones you intend to establish.
What this transaction does: no LEAP moves and no XRPL account setting changes. The normal XRP network fee applies. The Community ID and first Operating Wallet become canonical together only after Step 5 validates.
