What would this tell us?

This would show the growth of the organized LEAP community itself: how quickly new crew are joining, whether growth is steady or episodic, and how the community changes around launches, challenges, events, or voyages.

What could we compare it with?

Crew growth becomes more useful when viewed beside holders, reach, activity, achievements, or other signals. The prototype helps decide which relationships are meaningful enough to preserve.
