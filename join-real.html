Join the Crew

Ready to go from Leaper to Sailing Frog's Leap crew member? Joining the crew takes a few steps, and we'll walk you through each one. We'll help you get set up, send you your first LEAP Check, and when you cash it, you're officially a Sailing Frog's Leap Deckhand.

Step by Step

Everything you need and exactly what happens, in one place.

Phase 1

Get ready

Three steps get you ready for your first LEAP Check.

1

Open Xaman on your mobile device

Xaman is the wallet app we use to access and control your XRP Ledger account. If you already use Xaman, you are ready. If not, set it up first.

2

Keep a small amount of XRP in your XRP Ledger account

XRP pays the XRP Ledger's small network fees and satisfies the reserve requirements for your account.

You do not need much. A few XRP is plenty for LEAP.

3

Send us your public XRP Ledger address

Send Sailing Frog's Leap a DM on Instagram and tell us you want to join the Sailing Frog's Leap crew.

In Xaman, copy the public address for the XRP Ledger account you want to use for LEAP and include it in the DM. You do not need to post your address publicly.

Optional crew ritual: Take your first LEAP

Want to have some fun with it? Take a picture of yourself leaping into the air, post it on Instagram, and tag Sailing Frog's Leap. It is not required to join, but it can become part of your Sailing Frog's Leap crew story and badge history.

Nothing secret. We never need your recovery words, private key, family seed, or wallet secret.
You're ready for your LEAP Check
Phase 2

Claim your first LEAP

We send the Check. You verify it, cash it, and come aboard.

4

We send your first LEAP Check

When you send us your public XRP Ledger address by DM, we'll send your first LEAP Check to that address.

Your first Check is for 1 LEAP.

This is your invite to join the crew of Sailing Frog's Leap.

5

Open the Check in Xaman

Verify the Check before accepting it. Make sure it is for LEAP and was sent by the official Sailing Frog's Leap distributor wallet.

Official LEAP Distributor Address rnbmFoUKhnMZ8QCJ6kA5J2uwfP8rm9cUdU

The sending address must match this address exactly. If it does not match, do not cash the Check.

6

Cash the Check in Xaman

Approve the action in Xaman. The LEAP is credited to your XRP Ledger account and appears in Xaman.

Welcome aboard. You're a Sailing Frog's Leap Deckhand.

Your first LEAP establishes your Sailing Frog's Leap rank as Deckhand. After that, each LEAP you earn can come to you the same way: by Check.

You now hold your first LEAP. Your Sailing Frog's Leap crew story has started.
