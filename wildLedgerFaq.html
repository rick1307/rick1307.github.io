Wild Ledger FAQ

These are the rules and mechanics that apply across participating LEAP communities. Each community has its own identity and experience, but LEAP on the XRP Ledger is the common thread that connects them. This technology is still new to many people, so this FAQ is here to explain clearly what we’re building, how it works, and what stays the same in every Wild Ledger community you participate in.

Getting started

The basics

What is LEAP?LEAP is the membership and recognition token used across participating Wild Ledger communities. Each community decides what participation means inside its own community, while Wild Ledger provides the shared system that uses LEAP underneath.
Do I have to buy LEAP?No. LEAP is a membership and recognition token. When you earn LEAP, a participating community can offer it to you by LEAP Check.
Can I transfer LEAP to someone else?Yes. Checks are how participating communities can distribute LEAP from a distributor. Once you cash a Check, that LEAP is in your wallet and the Check has done its job. If transfers are enabled, you can send LEAP directly to another LEAP holder — keep it, share it with another member, or give some to someone new.
Is XRP the same as LEAP?No. XRP is the native asset of the XRP Ledger. LEAP is the membership and recognition token that lives on the XRP Ledger.
Is LEAP the same across participating communities?Yes. LEAP is the common token used across Wild Ledger communities. The communities themselves are different. Each participating community can create its own activities, recognition, badges, challenges, events, ranks, or other ways to take part.
Wallet, privacy & cost

Why Wild Ledger asks for an XRPL address

Your public address is not your wallet secret.

Wild Ledger only needs the public XRP Ledger address you choose to use for your Member Record. Wild Ledger and participating communities never need your recovery words, private key, family seed, or recovery phrase.

For privacy, a separate XRPL wallet for LEAP is recommended instead of your primary crypto wallet. Xaman also makes managing multiple XRP wallets easy.

Why does Wild Ledger need my XRP Ledger address?Your public XRPL address is how Wild Ledger knows which Member Record to load for you. Think of it as the number on your membership passport. Wild Ledger uses that address to read your LEAP balance, rank, badges, and history from the public XRP Ledger. You do not create a separate Wild Ledger username or password.
Is my XRPL address private?No. An XRPL address and the transactions associated with it are public on the XRP Ledger. That is an important thing to understand before you join.
If I give Wild Ledger my XRPL address, can someone see what that wallet has done?Yes. The XRP Ledger is public, so anyone who knows an XRPL address can view the public activity associated with that address. That is also why a separate wallet for LEAP activity is recommended. Wild Ledger does not need to know anything about your other crypto activity.
Is Wild Ledger building a database that connects my identity to my XRPL address?No. Wild Ledger uses the XRP Ledger as the external persistence layer for LEAP activity. A community Join page can ask for the public XRPL address you choose to use so your first LEAP Check can be processed. Your Member Record itself is built from public XRP Ledger data, not from a private Wild Ledger database of names and balances.
What personal information does Wild Ledger actually need from me?For the LEAP system, Wild Ledger needs the public XRPL address you choose to use. Wild Ledger does not need your private key, recovery phrase, banking information, government ID, or the address of your primary crypto wallet.
Why do you recommend a separate wallet for LEAP?A public blockchain address can reveal its public transaction history, and Wild Ledger does not need to know anything about your other crypto activity. A dedicated wallet for LEAP creates a clean boundary: your LEAP membership activity stays in one wallet, and everything else stays somewhere else.
Can Wild Ledger or a participating community access or control my wallet?No. Knowing your public XRPL address does not give anyone control of your wallet. You control it with your private keys. Never send Wild Ledger, a participating community, or anyone else your recovery words, private key, family seed, or recovery phrase.
Do I have to put a lot of XRP in this wallet?No. XRP pays the XRP Ledger's very small transaction fees and supports its reserve requirements. The current LEAP guidance is to keep enough XRP in the dedicated wallet to satisfy the XRP Ledger reserve and the small transaction costs needed to use LEAP. Reserve requirements can change, and XRP held for reserve is not a LEAP membership fee.
Why do you recommend Xaman?Xaman is currently the wallet Wild Ledger supports because it gives members a clear path through the XRP Ledger features used by the LEAP system, including enabling LEAP and cashing LEAP Checks. Xaman also makes managing multiple wallets easy, which supports a simple habit: keep long-term savings separate from the wallet you use for transactions. Supporting one wallet first also lets participating communities give everyone the same simple instructions.
Do I have to use Xaman?LEAP is built on the XRP Ledger, not on Xaman. Your Member Record is tied to your XRPL address. Xaman is simply the wallet Wild Ledger currently supports for the full LEAP process. Other XRPL wallets can be supported as their handling of the required XRP Ledger steps is verified.
Why not just use an email address and password?Because Wild Ledger is designed to use the XRP Ledger itself as the permanent logbook for LEAP activity. Your public XRPL address connects you to your Member Record without requiring a separate Wild Ledger username and password system. The community website is the front door, your LEAP record is the passport, and the XRP Ledger is the logbook.
LEAP Checks

About the Checks

What is a LEAP Check?A LEAP Check uses the XRP Ledger's built-in Check feature. It lets a participating community offer you LEAP without simply pushing tokens into your wallet. You choose whether to cash the Check and accept the LEAP.
Why use Checks instead of just sending LEAP?Checks let you choose whether to accept each LEAP award. They also give each award its own record on the XRP Ledger. Once you cash the Check, the LEAP is in your wallet and can move like any other LEAP.
Do I need to set up LEAP before I receive a Check?No separate LEAP setup is required before a community sends the Check. Xaman guides you through what is needed when you cash it.
Do I have to cash a Check?No. It is your wallet. A pending Check is an offer of LEAP. You choose whether to cash it and accept the LEAP. A LEAP Check can also carry the reason it was earned along with the LEAP itself, preserving that recognition as part of the XRP Ledger record.
How do I see LEAP Checks waiting for me?Your community's Member Record can list LEAP Checks that are currently waiting for you to cash. A Check remains outstanding until it is cashed or canceled.
How do I cash a Check?Wild Ledger currently supports Xaman for Check cashing. Your community can guide you to the Check in Xaman so you can review it and choose whether to accept it.
Member Record

Your LEAP record

What does my Member Record show?Your Member Record can show your current LEAP balance and rank, outstanding LEAP Checks waiting for you, and the LEAP history you have actually earned on the XRP Ledger.
What is LEAP Waiting for You?It is the list of LEAP Checks that are currently available for you to cash. A Check stays there while it is outstanding and disappears after it is cashed or canceled.
When does something become part of my LEAP history?A Check-based recognition becomes part of your earned record after that Check is successfully cashed. A Check that is still waiting or that was canceled does not count as earned.
What happens to old canceled Checks?They remain part of the public XRP Ledger transaction history, but they are not shown as earned badges or recognitions in your personal Member Record.
About LEAP

One more thing

Is LEAP an investment promise?No. LEAP is being introduced as a membership and recognition system. It is not a promise of profit or future monetary value.
